Privacy Policy

DoAide AutoApply (formerly TalentPing) — Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Who We Are

DoAide AutoApply (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an AI-powered job-application automation service available at job.doaide.com. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our service.

2. Data We Collect

When you sign up and use DoAide AutoApply, we may collect the following:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and Google account identifier used to sign in.
  • Gmail data: we request access to your Gmail account (via Google OAuth) to send application emails on your behalf and to read replies from recruiters. We access only the specific email threads related to your job applications.
  • Resume and profile data: resumes, cover letters, professional profiles, and job preferences you upload or provide.
  • Job application data: records of jobs you apply to, application status, recruiter responses, and follow-up correspondence.
  • Usage data: feature usage, page views, and interactions within the application for service improvement.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Automated job applications: we use your Gmail access to send tailored application emails and read recruiter responses so we can track and manage your applications.
  • AI-powered tailoring: your resume and profile data are processed by AI models to generate customized cover letters and application materials for each job posting.
  • Application management: we store application records to provide you with a dashboard showing statuses, responses, and follow-up actions.
  • Service improvement: aggregated, de-identified usage data helps us improve features and fix issues.

4. Gmail API — Limited Use Disclosure

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We only access Gmail data necessary to send application emails and read recruiter replies on your behalf.
  • We do not use Gmail data for advertising purposes.
  • We do not allow humans to read your email content unless you give explicit consent, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law.
  • We do not transfer Gmail data to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve the service, for security purposes, or as required by law.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • AI service providers: resume and job-posting content is sent to large-language-model providers to generate application materials. No Gmail content or credentials are shared with these providers.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • With your consent: for any other purpose, only with your explicit permission.

6. Data Retention

  • Your account data and application records are retained for as long as your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your personal data, including stored resumes, application history, and Gmail tokens, within 30 days.
  • De-identified, aggregated data may be retained for analytics purposes.

7. Data Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. Gmail OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and are never exposed to any third party.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Revoke Gmail access at any time through your Google Account permissions.
  • Export your application data.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or an in-app notice. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
